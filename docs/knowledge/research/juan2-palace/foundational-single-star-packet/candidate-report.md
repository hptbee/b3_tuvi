# Authored candidate research

This first tranche proposes four individual major-star opening annotations across three previously uncovered palaces. Thiên Lương in Thiên Di has a clear unqualified opening followed by Thiên Đồng and Thiên Cơ cohost variants; Thiên Lương in Nô Bộc similarly has a clear opening but preserves historical servant terminology and the contrary Thiên Cơ branch. Cự Môn in Thiên Di has a six-adverse intensifier, explicitly unevaluated. Thiên Cơ in Điền Trạch retains Cự Môn Mão/Dậu inheritance distinctions, Thiên Lương late development and Thái Âm self creation.

Exact candidate predicates require only direct target-star membership. Presence does not mean standalone; branch/brightness/absence of adversity are not asserted. Whether literal opening-only attribution remains valid across named cohosts is an unresolved semantic question for actual independent reviewers. Do not promote broad applicability to empirical interpretation accuracy.

The fixed 1,152-chart cohort currently gives 92 approved matches across four palaces. Hypothetically adding the four predicates sequentially gives 166, 251, 349 and 419 matches. This is reachability research, not delivered coverage. Per-palace counts, overlap and fixed-cohort identity are recorded in coverage-projection.json and the main coverage research.

Parents, children and health section openings require additional natal/opposite/triangular/brightness/day-night context. They are deferred; no claim is invented merely to fill a palace. Mệnh existing biographies also have brightness/year-stem/sex/adverse conditions, so this tranche does not flatten them. Disputed Thiên Phủ and T034 evidence remain unchanged.
