## K01 — 無正曜, 無星曜 and modern 空宮 need separate scopes

**Status:** COMPARED, `TERM_UNRESOLVED`, `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED` for original print scope.

**What is actually located:** Q3, **談星要論**, clause beginning **命宮無正曜**, after the ordering of natal, body and relational examination. Q2, **四子女**, introductory clause **本宮無星曜**, before individual star entries. I3's corresponding passage and I2's 子女 preamble retain comparable terms. X, `concepts.kong-gong`, defines the modern empty-palace condition by absence of a major star (主星). Y's contents use 十四正曜 and its prose uses 十四主星, supporting that author's modern roster usage; it does not define the classical preambles retroactively.

**Vietnamese paraphrase:** Q3 bàn trường hợp Mệnh không có chính diệu rồi xét cát tinh ở Tài và Quan; Q2 yêu cầu xem đối cung khi bản cung ở trường hợp được mô tả bằng “vô tinh diệu”. X định nghĩa Không cung theo việc không có chủ tinh.

**Governing context:** Q3 continues with adverse opposition, brightness, benefic assembly and broken-formation qualifications; Q2 proceeds through opposition, tam phương tứ chính and further distinctions. Neither short phrase alone supplies a complete judgment or an explicit fourteen-name roster for both terms.

**Comparison and research interpretation:** a software predicate “no member of the fourteen major stars” is a legitimate documented modern definition in X. It cannot establish that both classical phrases have identical operands. In particular, reading 無星曜 as literally “no stars of any class” or silently narrowing it to “no major stars” each requires source evidence. Within Y, 正曜/主星 appear as roster terminology, but author-specific usage is narrower evidence than universal historical equivalence.

**Unresolved / next:** obtain an identifiable original scan and a named commentary explicitly glossing each clause. Preserve the two classical terms separately in future transcriptions and translations. No runtime vô chính diệu change follows from this finding.

## K02 — 財官二宮 and 拱照 do not justify expanding to any benefic anywhere

**Status:** COMPARED, `TERM_UNRESOLVED`.

**Original locator:** Q3 **談星要論**, same conditional paragraph, anchor **財官二宮**. The specified palaces are part of the sentence's condition. X keys `ben-gong`, `san-he-wei`, `si-zheng-wei`, `san-fang-si-zheng`, `hui-zhao`, `tong-gong` describe modern relational vocabulary.

**Vietnamese paraphrase:** đoạn Q3 chỉ đích danh Tài và Quan trong trường hợp Mệnh vừa nêu, chứ không viết một điều kiện cát tinh có mặt ở bất cứ cung liên hệ nào. X tách đồng cung, vị trí tam hợp và đối cung, rồi dùng hội chiếu cho một tập quan hệ rộng hơn.

**Governing context / actual contrast:** X's tam hợp set includes the target and ±4 positions, its tứ chính entry denotes the opposite position, and its combined concept unites these. Its hội/chiếu discussion distinguishes the relation groups. R01's inspected helper similarly builds a four-position set, but this is a modern implementation. Q3's **explicit two-palace operand** cannot be replaced by that generic set merely because the heading involves relational judgment.

**Research interpretation:** retain both relation type and named endpoints when documenting a condition. General vocabulary helps explain geometry; it does not override a narrower clause. A benefic at the opposite palace or natal palace does not, by itself, demonstrate the particular 財官二宮 condition.

**Unresolved / next:** map 拱/照/會/夾 occurrences separately within a bounded original edition, then compare an independently authored Vietnamese explanation. 吉星's exact roster in this paragraph remains unresolved; R01's modern six-benefic classification is not its missing classical definition. This dossier does not resolve T019/T022 or T034.

## K03 — Thiên Phủ's mitigating language has limits and competing qualifications

**Status:** COMPARED, `TEXT_CONFLICT` / edition attribution unresolved.

**Original locators:** Q1, **諸星問答論 / 問天府所主若何**, including mitigating language followed by adverse opposition, empty/isolated conditions and a warning against uniform inference. V, **THIÊN PHỦ / 4. Ý nghĩa phúc thọ, tai họa**, and **ĐỊA KHÔNG, ĐỊA KIẾP / 6. Ý nghĩa và đặc điểm / Đồng cung với Tử, Phủ**.

**Vietnamese paraphrase of V:** Thiên Phủ được mô tả là có khả năng chế ngự một số sát tinh trong mức độ nhất định, nhưng trường hợp hội đủ bốn sao và các điều kiện Không Kiếp, Tuần Triệt làm thay đổi luận đoán. Phần Không Kiếp còn phản biện một cách đọc được gán cho Thái Thứ Lang.

**Context / comparison:** Q1 juxtaposes the star's mitigating role with later destructive conditions. V likewise prevents an unconditional mitigation reading, but supplies its own thresholds and editorial disagreement. V's inter-author assertion has not been checked against Thái Thứ Lang's original passage; it is evidence of what the attributed web text argues, not proof that the other author was accurately represented.

**Research interpretation:** the shared defensible observation is methodological: a favorable opening role does not erase subsequent qualifications. Agreement on that principle does not make the two accounts' exact adverse sets, thresholds or outcome wording interchangeable. The Vietnamese work is not merely counted as an independent translation of Q; its authorship and edition still require verification.

**Unresolved / next:** collate V against a lawful print extract, then inspect the directly cited Tân Biên passage. Preserve distinctions among same-palace, opposition, number of adverse stars and Không categories. Do not create a “Thiên Phủ neutralizes all sát tinh” claim.

## K04 — Tử Vi's imperial metaphor is not a guaranteed social outcome

**Status:** COMPARED, author-specific commentary scope.

**Original locators:** Q1 **諸星問答論 / 問紫微所主若何**, including attendants/cohosts and favorable/unfavorable contextual branches; Y printed pp.18–19, **十四正曜 / 紫微**. These are actually read passages, not an inference from a book title.

**Vietnamese paraphrase of Y:** tác giả dùng hình tượng đế tinh để mô tả khí chất và cách ứng xử, đồng thời xét vị trí và sao đi kèm; không thể suy thẳng rằng người có Tử Vi tất sẽ có địa vị cao. Tác giả còn tự điều chỉnh một nét mô tả trước đó vì thấy hợp với Thiên Đồng hơn.

**Context / comparison:** Y's spatial and cohost discussion constrains a generic personality label; its self-revision shows that even an author's concise characterization is interpretive and revisable. Q1 also requires accompanying context despite its imperial opening, but the classical passage and Y do not thereby provide an identical modern psychological theory.

**Research interpretation:** record metaphor, author commentary and conditional reasoning separately. A stable star name should not conceal a leap from traditional imagery to guaranteed occupation, rank or measurable personality. This is a useful synthesis-method caution, not a validated modern personality claim.

**Unresolved / next:** read the permitted full surrounding author chapter and compare a named Vietnamese author's Tử Vi section. The preview is insufficient for a complete Trung Châu synthesis or twelve-palace interpretation. Keep later 空-category chapters as contents-only leads.

## K05 — An opening palace/star sentence is subordinate to the section's preamble

**Status:** CONTEXT_MAPPED for the three bounded Q2 preambles; no independent Vietnamese preamble comparison completed.

**Original locators:** Q2 **四子女**, **六疾厄**, **十二父母**, before their 紫微 entries; I2 lines near 600, 663 and 817 respectively. The [palace index](PALACE_GAPS.md) records the different governing conditions without copying the text.

**Vietnamese paraphrase:** Tử Tức bắt đầu bằng bản cung, hung/sát và đối cung rồi mở rộng quan hệ; Tật Ách yêu cầu xét Mệnh và trạng thái sao trước khi luận các sao ở Tật; Phụ Mẫu liên hệ Nhật Nguyệt, sáng tối và ngày đêm sinh trước các mệnh đề riêng.

**Context / comparison:** these are not three interchangeable “one star in one palace” templates. Q3's ordering of natal/body/related palaces reinforces contextual reading within the same transmitted family; this is internal contextual support, not another independent witness.

**Research interpretation:** future star-cell notes must link their governing preamble and later exceptions. The first 紫微 sentence cannot be extracted as an unconditional family, fertility or health prediction. Traditional associations are historical interpretive material, not verified personal outcomes or modern advice.

**Unresolved / next:** retain original wording, exact scope and adverse/cohost conditions for each chosen cell; compare an independently authored and edition-identified source.
