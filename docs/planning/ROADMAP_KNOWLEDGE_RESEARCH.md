## Research scope and method

Build understanding before selecting executable rules. Preserve original wording, translation and editorial interpretation as distinct layers; record uncertainties and counterexamples alongside positive statements.

- **Stars:** major and minor stars, aliases in Vietnamese/traditional/simplified Chinese, classifications, placement versus meaning, miếu/vượng/hãm terminology and source-specific distinctions.
- **All twelve palaces:** Mệnh, Phụ Mẫu, Phúc Đức, Điền Trạch, Quan Lộc, Nô Bộc, Thiên Di, Tật Ách, Tài Bạch, Tử Tức, Phu Thê and Huynh Đệ. Treat Thân as an overlay, not a thirteenth palace. Index major-star/palace statements, section preambles, reciprocal entries and explicit absence of material.
- **Combinations and relations:** same-palace versus đối cung, tam hợp, hội/chiếu and giáp; named formations versus their actual operands; thành/phá cách and strengthening/weakening clauses. Reuse the [unresolved combinations](../knowledge/UNRESOLVED_COMBINATIONS.md) and [remediation evidence](../knowledge/RULE_REMEDIATION_BACKLOG.md), rather than duplicating their rows.
- **Modifiers and exceptions:** brightness, branch, stem, Cục, gender/age clauses, adverse/benefic stars, Không/Kiếp, Tuần/Triệt, cohosts and conditional precedence. An opening sentence is not a complete palace judgment; retain later qualifications and exclusions.
- **School differences:** compare named editions and attributed schools separately, including Tam Hợp, Tứ Hóa/Phi tinh and Trung Châu where readable material supports attribution. Extend research around the [existing variants reference](../tuvi/TU_VI_SCHOOL_VARIANTS.md); do not infer an author's school from a website label or blend incompatible profiles.
- **Interpretation methodology:** distinguish natal structure, star–palace meaning, relational synthesis, modifiers, exceptions and time-dependent reasoning. Record how each source weighs conflicts and uncertainty. Historical gender, servant, marriage, health and fatalistic language must remain contextualized source history, without becoming personal advice or factual outcomes.

For each topic: locate the full relevant section; establish edition and reuse scope; retain a byte-identified lawful artifact or permitted locator/excerpt; transcribe and translate with context; compare independent textual witnesses; document agreement, differences and unresolved terms; then request a separately scoped source review when ready. Useful collected or disputed material may remain non-executable indefinitely. Neither a search hit nor model consensus establishes historical authenticity or empirical validity.

## Source inventory strategy

Use [sources.json](../knowledge/sources.json) for stable source/edition identities and the existing [provenance contract](../knowledge/provenance-contract.json) and [claim model](../tuvi/KNOWLEDGE_CLAIM_MODEL.md) for meaning and evidence. Keep research packets under `docs/knowledge/research/<topic>/`; use existing passage, review and decision conventions when applicable. This roadmap owns prioritization, not a second source catalog or approval database. Candidate leads stay in a topic packet until registration is justified; do not add speculative records to runtime-consumed JSON merely to count them.

Inventory lanes:

1. **Classical Chinese books and editions.** Start from the registered Quanshu tradition and retained Juan2/Juan3 evidence. Seek identifiable scans, critical editions and library/catalog records to distinguish work, edition, transcription and commentary. A modern transcription or another mirror of it is not independent classical corroboration. Record inaccessible material as an access gap.
2. **Vietnamese books and ebooks.** Revisit the existing Vũ Tài Lục, Thái Thứ Lang, Nguyễn Phát Lộc and Thiên Lương leads in the [dated source register](../tuvi/TU_VI_SOURCE_REGISTRY.md). Separate catalog metadata, readable ebook, re-typeset PDF and original scan; reconcile attribution/page differences before comparison. These are research leads, not newly verified availability or permission claims.
3. **Chinese ebooks and school commentaries.** Identify actual author/editor, edition, language and stated lineage; prioritize readable context explaining the same pilot topics. Compare translation choices against Chinese wording without silently correcting the source. A named tradition without an inspected text stays a lead.
4. **GitHub repositories.** Reuse the pinned iztro source/software dossiers as the starting software reference; discover other independently authored repositories only where they add texts, terminology, algorithms or provenance. Pin commit/path, trace upstream dependencies and record code/data/text licenses separately. Software behavior is a modern implementation witness, not proof of a classical rule; forks and copied corpora count as one source family.
5. **Legally usable datasets.** Evaluate licensed star/palace glossaries, text corpora, bibliographic metadata and synthetic chart fixtures. Record origin, version, license, attribution, permitted extraction/redistribution and transformation history. Unknown permissions mean metadata/locator-only retention pending clarification, not bulk ingestion. Do not collect identifiable birth charts or personal life histories as a shortcut to validation; any such future work needs its own consent/privacy scope.

Every inventory entry should document, using existing fields or an accompanying research note: stable identity or provisional lead ID; title/author/editor/translator; language and edition; school attribution and its basis; URL/repository pin/file hash and actual access date/result; exact chapter/page/section locator; extraction method and OCR/image discrepancies; upstream source family; topic coverage; rights evidence and permitted use; known conflicts and next action. Unknown values remain unknown. Distinguish digital file identity, bibliographic identity and collation against an original. Preserve existing `authenticated` values and scopes; this strategy does not reinterpret or upgrade them.

Prefer usable, attributable and independent context over raw file count. Check rights per artifact and intended use: an old work's modern translation, scan or transcription can carry distinct restrictions, and a repository code license does not automatically license bundled books/data. Record the evidence for permissions; do not treat public accessibility as permission. This is an intake rule, not a claim that any named candidate is legally cleared.

## Research statuses and approval boundaries

Track **research progress** in each topic packet with these human-readable labels; they do not add enum values to runtime JSON:

- `LEAD`: bibliographic pointer only; text, identity or rights may be unavailable.
- `COLLECTED`: accessible artifact or permitted excerpt/locator retained, with provenance and known reuse limits; no semantic approval.
- `CONTEXT_MAPPED`: complete governing context, literal transcription, translation, aliases, modifiers and exceptions documented for the topic.
- `COMPARED`: independent source families compared with explicit agreements, differences and unresolved terminology. Comparison may reveal conflict.
- `REVIEW_READY`: exact source/condition/wording scope and required evidence frozen for a future source review; independently justified examples included where applicable. Non-executable scholarship need not invent chart fixtures.
- `SOURCE_REVIEWED`: actual source-review decision exists; report its exact verdict and limits, including disagreement or rejection. This label alone never means accepted.

Track `ACCESS_BLOCKED`, `RIGHTS_UNCLEAR`, `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `TEXT_CONFLICT` and `TERM_UNRESOLVED` as separate issue flags with a concrete next action. A blocked source does not block independent topics. Retain negative/disconfirming findings and prior versions rather than deleting them to improve metrics.

Maintain four independent dimensions on each topic summary:

1. **Collection/research:** the labels above and artifact access/reuse state.
2. **Source review:** exact accepted/limited/disagreed/unavailable result, edition/profile and immutable decision references. Use the existing independent source-review/comparator policy when acceptance is sought; a comparison note is not a fabricated reviewer judgment. An accepted review of one transcription is not multi-source corroboration.
3. **Runtime approval:** link an existing exact runtime claim/binding if one exists; otherwise `not_requested` or `not_approved`. Source-reviewed scholarship does not automatically become executable, and runtime-engineering work is outside this roadmap's present scope.
4. **Public-release permission:** retain existing withheld/closed gates independently of source or runtime acceptance. Rights to retain a text are also distinct from permission to publish personalized interpretation. No permission changes are made by this document.

Use existing stored decision vocabulary verbatim; these documentation labels neither replace the provenance layers nor weaken historical authentication, expert-verification or public-release gates.

Initial documentation audit establishes 14 registered sources/five editions and the recorded engineering figures above; the research-depth metrics below were initially **not baselined**; the dated Batch 1–4 snapshots above now supply bounded denominators. Do not fill them from filenames or historical headline counts.

- **Inventory accountability:** artifacts with recorded access, identity, pin/hash where available and rights state / all inventoried artifacts; separately report usable, inaccessible and rights-unclear totals. First inventory target: 100% explicit states, not 100% permission or authenticity.
- **Independent breadth:** distinct readable textual source families, editions, languages and evidenced school profiles per topic. Report mirrors, shared upstream texts and software references separately. Pilot target: two independent textual families where available, with gaps shown rather than forced agreement.
- **Palace and star depth:** context-mapped palace sections /12 and explicitly indexed major-star/palace cells /168; separately count cells with two-family comparison, modifiers, exceptions and unresolved terms. Indexing every cell does not mean every cell has source-reviewed knowledge. Minor-star expansion uses a separately named, bounded catalog denominator.
- **Traceability and contextual completeness:** reviewed dossier statements with exact source/edition/locator, original span or permitted reference, translation, governing context and retained modifiers/exceptions / all statements in the reviewed batch. Target every statement traceable or explicitly marked unsupported; extraction count is not semantic coverage.
- **Disagreement visibility:** compared topics with agreement, variant, conflict, missing source and missing terminology, plus closure reasons and reopened cases. Do not optimize for a high acceptance rate or count unresolved cases as failures to be hidden.
- **Review quality:** exact source decisions by verdict and scope, independent reviewer evidence and fixture provenance where applicable; distinguish source review from historical authentication, expert verification and empirical validation. No count alone grants approval.
- **Engineering and release separation:** retain runtime claim counts, fixed-cohort applicability/overlap/per-palace coverage and public permissions as separate columns when reported. The recorded 56.16% is a regression/applicability observation; increasing it is not this phase's success criterion. No new runtime measurements or approval changes were performed for this roadmap adoption.

A batch is complete when its question and searched corpus are bounded, retained material has provenance/rights states, contextual comparisons and negative findings are traceable, unresolved issues have next actions, and existing frozen evidence/decisions are preserved. Full knowledge completeness, historical authenticity and prediction validity must not be inferred from this research completion condition.
